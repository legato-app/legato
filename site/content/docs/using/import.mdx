---
title: "Import your music"
description: "Folders, YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer and SoundCloud."
---

- A folder: Settings › Accounts adds it; Legato reads the tags on a few threads and only rescans files that changed. Files without tags are matched by their name.
- YouTube Music: sign in to Google on its own page, in an app window. Legato never sees your password.
- Spotify: Connect Spotify opens Exportify (a free open-source tool) in a window, then Legato takes in the file it makes. Or paste a public playlist link.
- Apple Music, Deezer, SoundCloud: paste a public playlist, album or song link anywhere in the app.

Imported songs are played from YouTube, or from your files when you own them. Legato matches by ISRC first, then title, artist and length. Settings › Privacy lists what each import asked of which site.
