---
title: "First steps"
description: "The welcome, the search and the menus."
---

The welcome offers the ways in: your music folders, YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music or a link. Skip any of them; Settings › Accounts has them all later.

- Press Ctrl+K and search once: results come from every place you connected, merged, one row per song.
- Right-click anything: every surface has its own menu (play next, add to a playlist, edit song info, credits).
- The player bar at the bottom keeps the queue, the volume, lyrics and the Now Playing page one click away.
